**VolleyServe.it** is the site that collects the software projects **napo** builds for volleyball, shared in the spirit of the open source community.

**napo** splits his time between volleyball - a coach for 30 years and a federal instructor - and his work as a researcher at Fondazione Bruno Kessler.

More information: [Maurizio Napolitano](https://www.linkedin.com/in/napolnx/)
