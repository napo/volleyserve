**VolleyServe.it does not set any cookies** — neither for profiling nor for statistics.

The site is static: there are no forms sending data to external servers, no tracking scripts, no social sharing buttons loading third-party code.

Even the fonts and libraries used in the interface (icons, layout) are hosted directly on this domain: no request is sent to Google Fonts or any other external service while browsing.

The one exception is the PayPal donation button at the bottom of the page: clicking it takes you off this site, to PayPal's own domain, which has its own privacy policy and cookie handling, independent of VolleyServe.it.

If this ever changes — for example if a statistics tool is added — this page will be updated accordingly.
